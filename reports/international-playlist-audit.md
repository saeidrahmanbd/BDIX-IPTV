- Organizer is run by maintenance before the International playlist validator.
- Non-Bangladesh news channels are removed; Bangladesh news is kept.
- Non-Islamic religious channels are removed. Recognized Islamic religious channels remain in `Religious`.

- Alternate distinct streams for repeated channel identities are moved to `Backups`; exact duplicate URLs are omitted.
