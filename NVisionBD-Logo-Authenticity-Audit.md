# NVisionBD Playlist Logo Authenticity Audit

Date: 2026-10-11

## Policy
Only use a channel logo when it can be matched to the broadcaster's own website, official press/media assets, or a reliable source that clearly identifies the exact channel. Do not invent/recreate artwork and label it as official. Unverified artwork is removed from playlist use until a match is verified.

## Changes made
- Removed 42 playlist logo references that pointed to bespoke SVG artwork that had not been verified as broadcaster-provided.
- Removed the corresponding unverified custom SVG assets from `logos/`, including two remaining PNG/JPG variants.
- Retained the playlist's channel entries and stream URLs.
- Verified that the playlist has 561 entries, no missing local logo-file references among the entries that still have a logo, and no external logo URLs.
