Bangladeshi IPTV & Bangla IPTV M3U Playlists

BDIX-IPTV brings together organized M3U/M3U8 IPTV playlists featuring television channels from Bangladesh and India, including a wide selection of Bangla TV channels, alongside a separate international collection of movie and music channels. The playlists are maintained with channel metadata, repository-hosted logo assets and a shared XMLTV Electronic Programme Guide (EPG), supported by automated checks and regular updates. How the playlists are maintained

The project separates published channel groups from discovery candidates. New primary channels are placed in New Channels, while alternate stream candidates are held in New Backups for manual review. Automated checks help validate playlist structure, metadata and stream responses, but an automated discovery result is not a guarantee that a stream will remain available.

The International playlist follows a published country policy: Bangladesh channels of all genres are eligible; Indian News, Religious, Educational and Kids channels are excluded while Indian Sports are allowed; other countries are limited to dedicated movie and music channels.

What does BDIX-IPTV provide?

BDIX-IPTV organizes public M3U playlist references, channel names and IDs, category metadata, local logo assets, XMLTV EPG mappings, stream-health reports and backup reviews. The repository is maintained on GitHub and its playlist files are served from the repository's raw-content URLs.

Network and availability note: “BDIX” describes the project focus. It is not a guarantee that every listed stream is hosted inside every Bangladeshi ISP's BDIX network. Stream reachability and quality depend on each provider, network route, region and player.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a Bangladeshi IPTV or Bangla IPTV playlist?

Yes. The main M3U collection includes Bangladesh channels and Indian channels. The international list also includes Bangladesh and India, with the remaining countries limited to movie and music channels.

Does BDIX-IPTV host TV channels or video?

No. The repository publishes playlist files containing stream references and related metadata. It does not host broadcaster channels or video content.

Does every channel have a working stream?

No playlist can guarantee uninterrupted access to every provider stream. Automated checks are indicators; access can vary by provider, network, location and time.

What is the EPG link?

The XMLTV programme guide is published at epg/epg.xml after a successful refresh and validation.
